# Leonardo: conversation analysis and demo direction

Prepared 11 September 2026. Sources: the user-supplied conversation DOCX and pasted profile, plus the official public company pages linked below. This preparation document is not served by the app.

## What the conversation establishes

Leonardo explicitly accepted the proposed short walkthrough. That is permission to demonstrate the idea, not evidence of a budget, purchase intention, internal sponsor mandate or a request to implement a system.

His stated decision signals were unusually specific: the customer purchased only special products with high added value, was growing, and had potential if supported. He described three or four similar experiences. Those are useful examples of his judgment, not enough evidence to estimate a success probability or prove that support caused retention.

The original event involved absorbing a claimed loss in installments despite the material having no identified defect. The previous 7% price-concession example captured the longer-term theme but changed the actual decision mechanism. The revised lead case therefore uses a fictional one-time settlement. Its $350K amount, customer identity, margins, tonnage and installment schedule are invented; the actual customer and amount were not disclosed.

He did not confirm that Grupo Curimbaba currently suffers from fragmented systems. That was the possibility raised in your message. Present the workspace as an illustration of how evidence could be connected, then ask what actually reaches decision-makers today.

## A 15-minute walkthrough

| Time      | Show                           | Purpose                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–2 min   | Overview and Atlas spotlight   | State that the example is fictional and inspired by the decision logic discussed.                                                                                             |
| 2–5 min   | Atlas Account 360              | Connect specialty purchases, growth, contribution and notes. Invite him to identify missing signals.                                                                          |
| 5–8 min   | Decision brief                 | Show the synthesis and the line between evidence, assumptions and executive judgment.                                                                                         |
| 8–11 min  | Relationship-support simulator | Change installments from 12 to 24: cash timing changes, economic cost does not. Set volume uplift to zero: support must then be justified by something beyond modeled growth. |
| 11–13 min | Business question and radar    | Compare relationships using the same records; inspect why each receives its position.                                                                                         |
| 13–15 min | Executive review notes         | Ask which evidence he would need before taking this to an executive or advisory discussion.                                                                                   |

Suggested opening: “I built a small fictional industrial-minerals example around the logic you described: specialty purchases, customer growth, and whether absorbing a short-term loss could protect long-term value. I’d like your view on whether it brings the right evidence together.”

Useful closing questions: Which signal is missing? Where does that information reach leadership today? Who should validate the technical and financial assumptions before a decision? Keep the conversation exploratory, consistent with the walkthrough invitation.

If no API key is configured, say that the current demonstration uses calculated local analysis. Do not describe it as a live LLM session or a deployed client implementation. The optional server-side AI connection needs credentials and an end-to-end check before presenting it as live.
